seed 7, 400-step cap) from `native_pi0_libero90/`, labelled from 24-frame
contact sheets and enlarged frames by six parallel reviewers (2026-10-08).

proposed (N*, G*, L1, T1, and R5/R6 in reviewer 5's notes) were later merged
into R5-R7 and S4 (summary, section 2). Image left/right are mirrored relative
to the instruction, since the camera faces the robot.
