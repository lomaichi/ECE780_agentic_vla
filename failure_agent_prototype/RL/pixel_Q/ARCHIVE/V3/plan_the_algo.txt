6. DECISIONS SO FAR (2026-11-22, from the comments above and chat)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Train V (0a). REVISED: actor/learner loop (user, after the video question): actors run the rollouts, the
  learner trains V online and sends its weights back, so videos show the current V's V_est. train_v.py stays
  for offline refits from the saved shards.
- Main rollout ends on pick success, timeout, or irrecoverable failure (bowl off the table / pushed > 30 cm /
  sim error; counted as "not picked"). No "no gated tick for 1 s" condition. Timeout: the 20 s LIBERO-90 budget
  (400 control steps).
- Gate (0c): pick not done; gripper open (last command open, finger gap >= 5 cm); target within 10 cm (xy) of
  the grasp point (3 cells x 2 cm + 4 cm); gripper empty. A gated tick also needs: no HL correction in the last
  3 s, agentview sees >= 20 target pixels. No tilt condition.
  REVISED (2026-11-22): height condition added: grasp point (eef) at most 10 cm above the target's top
  (--gate-max-above-cm 10; < 0 = off). In the first v3 shards, gated ticks > 12 cm above the top were ~1/3 of
  the samples with a 4-11% pick rate within 3 s (vs 19-27% below 9 cm).
- Flags: --tick-steps 4 (5 fps), --p-select 0.5, --n-perturb 5, --pos-cm 1.5, --dz-cm -0.5 1.5, --rot-deg 10,
  --move-cap 30, --h-max-s 3.0, --target-samples 10000 (branch episodes kept; est. 45-75 min on 6 workers),
  --workers 6, --hl privileged, --video-every 50. Seed fixed at 0 (no flag). Bump threshold 5 mm (no flag).
- Natural branch episode at selected ticks: KEPT (ep 0 = pi0 alone from the unperturbed snapshot).
- Main-rollout labels: SKIPPED (only branch episodes, natural + perturbed, are V samples).
- Bumped / unarrived perturbed episodes: not saved (only counted for wandb rates).
- No --save-cloud; synthetic-observation check is a separate stand-alone test (not in this code).
- No V2 data reused.
- Task split 7:3: train on all 14 training-split bowl/mug tasks; collect eval data on 6 held-out bowl/mug
  tasks (13, 15, 24, 36, 65, 76; flag --eval-tasks). Held-out data only reports test metrics: never trains V,
  never picks the best epoch (val = tag split within the 14 training tasks). Eval target 4300 samples
  (3/7 of 10000; flag --eval-target-samples).
- Outputs: data/shard_*.npz, config.json (collection); v_cnn.pt, metrics.json (trimmed: MSE, R2, AUC, bias on
  all / natural / perturbed + calibration bins), config.json (training).
- wandb as in section 3.
- Videos: every 50th training rollout, rendered by the actor with the learner's current weights (V_est at every
  gated tick and at each episode's takeover pose).


7. FINAL DESIGN (code: V3/episodes.py, V3/train.py; train_v.py reads the shards)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rounds (as V2): each round runs --workers rollouts in parallel, then the learner updates.
  - job type: eval rollout (held-out task) while eval samples < 3/7 of all and < --eval-target-samples,
    else training rollout. Starts drawn from shuffled cycles over (task, init).
  - training starts: tag-hash split, 15% of (task, init) tags = val (never trained on; picks the best V).
  - every --video-every training rollouts: that job records a video and gets the current V weights.
Actor rollout: pi0 + privileged HL from the init state; tick every 4 steps; at a gate-open tick: observe
  (grounded check); selected with p 0.5 -> snapshot -> ep 0 natural + 5 perturbed (move, observe at arrival,
  pi0 until pick or 3 s) -> restore. Ends: pick success, 20 s budget, irrecoverable failure.
  Kept samples: natural episodes + perturbed episodes that arrived and didn't bump (grounded at takeover).
Learner: V ensemble (vnet.py cnn, 5 heads, bootstrap 0.8), masked MSE on gamma^t labels (gamma 0.85, H 3 s),
  --natural-weight; after each round utd 0.5 x new training samples gradient steps (batch 64) once >= 300
  samples. Every --eval-every 50 rollouts: val MSE (saves v.pt when it improves) + test metrics on the eval
  tasks (all / natural / perturbed: MSE, R2, AUC, bias) -> wandb + stdout.
Outputs (--out-dir): data/shard_*.npz, config.json, v.pt (best val), metrics.json (final, best v.pt: test
  metrics + calibration bins), reliability.png, curves.png, videos/*.mp4, wandb/ (only with --wandb-project).
Learner flags (train_v.py defaults, not discussed): --width 32 --heads 5 --bootstrap-p 0.8 --lr 3e-4
  --weight-decay 1e-4 --batch 64 --utd 0.5 --warmup 300 --val-frac 0.15 --eval-every 50 --device cpu
  (LIBERO venv torch can't use the RTX 5090).
